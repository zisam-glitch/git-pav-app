A Legacy of Care and Innovation
Our dental clinic isn't just about delivering exceptional oral healthcare; it's a testament to the vision
of the late Dr. Pavan Bhogal. Dr. Pav was a passionate believer that dentistry could – and should – extend
beyond traditional practice, providing unparalleled convenience and accessibility.
The Dream and The Team

Dr. Pav's dream was shared, nurtured, and diligently planned alongside his close friend and colleague, Dr. Hassan. Together, they meticulously crafted a plan, encompassing these crucial steps:

Dream : Envisioning a patient-centered clinic where the best dental care meets the flexibility of mobile services.
Creating a Partnership : Building a foundation of shared passion and complementary expertise.
Reaching Shared Value between the Partnership : Defining their commitment to patient well-being and professional excellence.
Creating a Very Detailed Roadmap : Outlining every stage, from cutting-edge technology acquisition to community outreach.

A Legacy Fulfilled
Tragically, Dr. Pav passed away before witnessing the clinic's realization. Their shared vision now rests with Dr. Hassan as a profound responsibility. Fueled by a deep respect for their collaboration, Dr Hassan vowed to complete the final step:

Executing : Bringing this dental clinic to life, serving our community with the same care and innovation Dr. Pav championed.

Inspiration and Growth : "We can achieve all our dreams if we are brave enough to pursue them." This philosophy, shared by Dr. Pav and Dr. Hassan, will forever guide our practice.

While we must forgive past failings and present obstacles, the future of this clinic demands unwavering standards. We will never compromise on the quality, compassion, or dedication to access that Dr. Pav envisioned.

We invite you to join us on this journey, as we honor a remarkable legacy and build a future where dental care is truly accessible to all.
The Future of Dentistry

We believe the Pav Dental App, alongside our Hub and Mobile clinic, represents the future of dentistry. It's a future shaped by Dr. Pav's belief in accessibility and patient empowerment.

We're proud to announce the Pav Dental App – think of it as the Uber of dentistry! With just a few taps, you can:

Schedule routine checkups and cleanings at your home or office.
Book emergency appointments for swift relief.
Consult virtually with our dentists for immediate advice.
