# Audit: shared shell (header, cookie banner, footer) and Home page

Measured on http://localhost:8180 at 1440 and 390 px. Hims reference: `01-hims-live-urlbox-fullpage/home.png`. Not checked: the pictures' crops at 390 px by eye; hover states; the mobile drawer's full open state (only its link sizes were measured).

## Footer seam: exact cause

`website/src/components/home/footer.tsx` line 43: `<footer className="rounded-t-xl bg-black ...">` (24 px top radius) sits on a white page (`body` and `main` are white). Two things combine:

1. Pages that end with a closing band (`CtaBand`, a rounded `bg-brown` #453421 or `bg-navy` #1c3e56 card inside a `wrap section-y` section) leave the section's 80 px bottom padding as a white strip. Result: coloured rounded band, 80 px white gap, black rounded-top card. Measured: `/` band bottom 9689, footer top 9769; `/about`, `/services`, `/faq`, `/contact-us` brown; `/team` navy (#1c3e56, the "navy band" the owner saw).
2. Pages that end with a dark section flush against the footer (gap 0) show white wedges in the footer's two rounded top corners, because the corners reveal the white page: `/pricing` (brown, flush), `/services/dentures` (navy-deep #0b2029, flush; this is navy touching black with white corner slivers).
Pages with a light last section (whitening, emergency-dental, shop, blog, petition, vacancies, legal, cart) show a white-to-black rounded card, which is the Hims look and is fine. Every page uses the same Footer (about 40 routes), so one fix covers all.

The large sentence ("Leading cosmetic and dental care includes...") is `FOOTER.about` in `content/site-links.ts`, rendered as `text-h3` (about 28 px) at the top of the black card.
