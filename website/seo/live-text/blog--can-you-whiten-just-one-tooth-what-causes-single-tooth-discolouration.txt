Can You Whiten Just One Tooth? What Causes Single-Tooth Discolouration?
By Dr Bhojani
September 22, 2026

It can be surprising when most of your teeth look fairly white but one tooth looks yellow, grey, or darker.

This can happen because of multiple reasons, some of which may only require stain removal, but a single darker tooth may also have alternative underlying causes requiring professional intervention.

This is important as normal bleaching procedures will not always remove single tooth discolouration.

Can Dentists Whiten Just One Tooth?

Yes, in some cases, a dentist can fix the cosmetic problem of one discolored tooth, but the most appropriate way of treatment depends on the reason for the changes

Therefore, if the colour change occurred on the outside of the tooth, standard teeth whitening can solve the problem. However, in case of internal damage, more complex methods should be used

This is why it is so important to consult with a dentist before deciding to lighten your teeth

In case you are looking for professional teeth whitening in Bradford, you can discuss this issue with your dentist and decide what the best option for single-tooth discoloration would be.

What Causes Single-Tooth Discolouration?

There are a number of factors that could be the cause.

Some are linked with lifestyle while others can occur as a result of trauma, decay or internal problems in the tooth.

Being able to identify the cause is the first step for finding a suitable treatment.

1. Previous Tooth Injury

A tooth can sometimes darken as a result of an injury.

For example, you may have hit your tooth at some point, maybe as a child or in a sports accident. Even if it happened years ago, the affected tooth can suddenly start to look darker.

There may have been some internal damage to the tooth structure as a result of the trauma. A dentist can assess this and decide whether it might be contributing to the change in colour.

2. A Tooth That Has Undergone a Canal Treatment

A tooth that has had root canal treatment can sometimes darken over time.

This is a result of the tooth no longer having the same living tissue in it as a healthy tooth

The colour change can make one tooth stand out from the others in your smile.

Sometimes it can be possible to internally whiten a tooth that has had root canal treatment, and this is different to the more common external whitening of the teeth.

3. Tooth Decay

A dark or discoloured tooth may indicate decay.

Decay can often lead to brown; black or grey spots on the tooth and can result in tooth sensitivity and/or pain.

Tooth whitening is not recommended as a method to mask decay.

Decay needs to be addressed and treated before any cosmetic bleaching should be done to prevent further damage to the teeth.

4. Staining From Food and Drinks

Sometimes it can be much more simple than you think.

Coffee, tea, red wine, curry, etc. are examples of highly pigmented beverages and foods that can cause staining. Usually they appear on several teeth, not just one.

But sometimes a tooth can have more intense staining on it compared to others, as it has absorbed more of the pigment.

A dentist can usually remove these kinds of staining through a professional cleaning.

5. Dental Fillings or Other Restorations

A tooth may appear dark because it has an old filling or another type of restoration.

This is an important point to consider since whitening products only whiten natural tooth structure. They will not whiten most existing fillings, crowns, or other types of restoration in the same way.

As a result, whitening multiple teeth can sometimes cause an old restoration to stand out.

Your dentist can advise you whether replacing or changing the restoration would be more appropriate.

6. Natural Differences Between Teeth

Not all teeth naturally have the same shade.

One may be slightly darker or more yellow due to thinner enamel or the structure of the underlying enamel.

This may not be abnormal.

If the colour has always been slightly different and the tooth is otherwise healthy, your dentist may be able to talk to you about cosmetic options if you're interested in having them match more closely.

7. Age-Related Changes

Teeth often tend to darken with age.

The enamel becomes thinner, allowing the darker dentine underneath to show through.

If you have one tooth that is slightly a different colour to the rest, this can sometimes create a noticeable contrast.

Your dentist will be able to advise you on whether normal bleaching is likely to help.

Why Regular Whitening May Not Work On Just One Tooth

This is a very relevant question that is often overlooked.

Traditional teeth whitening procedures are designed to brighten natural teeth, but they cannot effectively mask or counteract all forms of internal discoloration

Therefore, if your tooth has darkened due to trauma, root canal therapy, decay, or restoration, chances are you will be disappointed by the results of any standard whitening treatment you attempt to use on it.

The treatment must take into account the reason behind the change in color of the affected tooth.

What Can a Dentist Do About One Dark Tooth?

The treatment will depend on the findings of the examination.

If there is a surface stain on the tooth then cleaning or normal whitening may help

If it is due to staining of the tooth structure, then your dentist may talk to you about other cosmetic treatments. In some cases, internal whitening may be an option for a tooth which has had root treatment and is suitable.

For some teeth, a veneer or crown may be discussed if whitening is unlikely to produce the shade desired.

Choosing the Right Whitening Treatment

There is no universal tooth whitening treatment, suitable for all types of discolouration.

Professional teeth whitening in Bradford can be an effective solution where the shade alteration is appropriate for whitening, but a single darker tooth may require a more specific approach.

A dental examination can help to identify whether whitening, cleaning, internal treatment or other cosmetic option would be most appropriate.

Final Thoughts

A discoloured tooth doesn't mean that you need more whitening.

It could be a surface stain or the result of an injury, root canal treatment, a cavity, a filling, or just a genetic trait.

Therefore, it's best to get it checked first before trying to change its colour.

FAQs
Can a dentist whiten only one tooth?

Yes, just in case your teeth respond to normal whitening otherwise you may require internal whitening or a different kind of cosmetic treatment.

Why is one tooth darker than the others?

There could be multiple reasons like old injury, root canal treatment, decay, staining, an old filling, or a structural difference in the tooth.

Will normal whitening work on a dark tooth?

Surfaces that have been stained often respond well to whitening, while teeth that have been damaged from within may need to be treated differently.

Can a root-treated tooth be whitened?

Depending on the condition of the tooth, in some cases a dentist may attempt an internal bleaching to whiten a root-treated tooth.

Should I see a dentist if one tooth suddenly changes colour?

Yes, sometimes the sudden darkening of a tooth means there is something wrong on the inside, even if there is no pain. Having the tooth examined can help find the right reason.
